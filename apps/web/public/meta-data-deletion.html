User Data Deletion / Kullanıcı Verilerinin Silinmesi

To delete your data from Lerta (Lerta Messaging Hub, Social Hub) or revoke Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) access:

  1. In Lerta: Account → Social media → Connections → disconnect the channel.
  2. On Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites → remove Lerta Messaging Hub or Lerta Social Hub.
  3. Email a deletion request from your registered address to lerta@lerta.com.tr with subject "Veri silme talebi" / "Data deletion request".

We respond within 30 days after identity verification.
